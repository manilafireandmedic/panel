What FMD
does.

One department. Multiple disciplines. Our services are organized around protecting life, responding to emergencies, and keeping the community informed.

01 / RESPONSE

Fire & Rescue

Respond to fires, rescue incidents, and other emergencies with trained personnel and coordinated operations.

02 / MEDICAL

Emergency Medical Care

Provide urgent medical response and support during emergency situations.

03 / SAFETY

Public Safety

Support safer communities through readiness, prevention, and responsible emergency service.

04 / TEAM

One Department

Fire, medical, and operational teams work together under a shared standard of service.

Mission

To provide quality service and prompt responses to the people of Manila, with personnel available for Medical and Fire services at all hours of the day (24/7).

Vision

To ensure we provide quality service and carry out our governmental responsibilities. Our motto: “To Serve and Rescue.”
